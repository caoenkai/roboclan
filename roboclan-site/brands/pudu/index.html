The Story

How PUDU got here.

Founded in 2016 by Felix Zhang, an alumnus of the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology, PUDU set out to put service robots to work in the places people actually eat, stay and heal — not in labs, but on restaurant floors and in hotel corridors.

Its cat-faced delivery robots became a familiar sight in dining rooms worldwide, and the lineup grew from food running into commercial cleaning, industrial handling and, more recently, embodied-intelligence and humanoid platforms.

Today PUDU is one of the world's largest commercial service robotics companies, with a fleet deployed across more than 60 countries and a reported share of roughly a quarter of the global market.

PUDU's bet was never on a demo — it was on the floor. Its robots earn their keep in real restaurants, hotels and hospitals, day after day.
